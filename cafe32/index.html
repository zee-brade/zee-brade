OUR STORY

From a local kitchen to a Viennese morning.

Café Website began in 2019 with a simple obsession, making mornings feel intentional. What started as a tiny kitchen in Vienna’s 7th district grew into a warm neighborhood spot where coffee is served with care, pastries are baked daily, and brunch arrives like a soft ritual.

We believe in good beans, flaky layers, honest ingredients, and the quiet luxury of a table that asks nothing from you except to stay a little longer.

“A café should feel like a pause button with better lighting.”
Crafted in Neubaugasse, loved by early risers, pastry seekers, and slow storytellers.
