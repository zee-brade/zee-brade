Zeebooth – frame layout update

Frame backgrounds now support two methods:
1. Editor upload: open Final Frame > Frame background, choose Upload JPG on any of the 4 slots.
2. assets fallback: place frame-bg-1.jpg through frame-bg-4.jpg inside /assets.

Uploaded images are resized/compressed and stored locally in the browser so they stay available after reload. Use the “Assets” button on an uploaded slot to revert that slot to its /assets file.

Gesture mode is indicator-only; it never triggers the shutter automatically.
